The district is playing semantic games with public records requests and burying their response to community concerns. We have built a bulletproof paper trail. Now we are moving the leverage completely out of their hands — into arenas where they cannot ignore us.

What's at stake
▸Aviation High's national flight programs are stalling. The forced removal of leadership with vital industry connections has fractured industry trust — and without that trust, the program fails.

Our public challenge to Dr. Duran

"The district claims they are bound by confidentiality. We challenge them to immediately waive any non-disclosure agreements or directives keeping Principal Tipton from speaking to this community.

Share this challenge everywhere: Facebook community groups, Nextdoor, school parent networks.

For the record

Staff have already told this community that his unannounced appearance at the school — in the middle of this situation — felt like intimidation. His presence at graduation, after being made aware of that, was not neutral. It was a choice. We are putting it on the record: that choice felt hostile and cruel to the educators and families who were there.
